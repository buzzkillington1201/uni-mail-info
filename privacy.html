Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal UniMail Gmail integration and this information website.

Google account access

UniMail requests Gmail read-only access after the account owner authorizes it through Google. It reads message content and metadata, including subjects, senders, recipients, dates, labels and identifiers, to organize mail and identify actions, payment requests and dates. It does not use this permission to send, delete or alter Gmail messages.

Processing and storage

Message copies, extracted details, feedback and calendar entries are stored on the owner's computers. Locally hosted models process message text to extract useful information. The configured Uni app accesses these services through authenticated network endpoints and may keep cached copies on the device. Recovery backups can retain additional copies.

Sharing and notifications

UniMail is not an advertising service and does not sell Gmail data. When urgent-mail push notifications are enabled, Google Firebase Cloud Messaging processes notification content and device delivery identifiers. Content can include an email subject, sender, extracted action, date or amount. Notification previews may be visible on the configured device. Events explicitly added to Uni's calendar can be read by configured calendar subscribers; anyone holding its secret subscription link can access that feed.

Use of Google data

Google user data is used to provide the personal mail organization, reminder and calendar features described here. It is not used to train generalized AI models. UniMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and your choices

There is no automatic retention deadline for stored mail copies. The owner controls deletion of local mail, extracted data, device caches and recovery backups. Revoking Google access prevents future authorized access but does not erase previously stored copies. Done, paid and filtering actions in Uni change Uni's records only. The owner can revoke the integration through their Google Account connections settings.

This website and contact

These static pages contain no mail, login form, analytics scripts or advertising trackers added by UniMail. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses for delivery and security under its own policies. For access, deletion or privacy questions, use the support contact displayed on the Google consent screen.
