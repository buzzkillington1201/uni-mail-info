Uni / personal mail companion

Your important mail.
With its context.

UniMail is the personal Gmail integration in Uni. It helps its owner review actionable messages, payment requests and dates, with quoted evidence from the original email.

What it does

  • Reads authorized Gmail messages and stores a copy on the owner's computers.
  • Uses locally hosted models and rules to extract and organize useful details.
  • Offers feedback, hidden-message review and personal done/paid notes.
  • Adds an event to the separate Uni calendar when the owner explicitly chooses to do so.
  • Can deliver reminders and selected urgent-mail notifications to a configured device.

Gmail access is read-only. UniMail does not send, delete or modify messages in Gmail. Its classifications and extracted dates can be wrong; check the original message before acting.

Personal use

UniMail is operated for its owner's personal use. This website provides information about the integration; it does not offer public mailbox access, account registration or a hosted mail service.

Read the privacy policy and terms of use. For questions, use the support contact shown on the Google consent screen.
